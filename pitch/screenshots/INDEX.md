# Figure library (menu for the pitch)

Generated by `.venv/Scripts/python eda/09_figures.py`. Every figure exists as `.png` and `.svg`, light and `-dark`. **This is a menu to choose from: nothing here is wired into `pitch/PITCH.md`.** The project isn't finished, so numbers will move; re-run the script after each new result.

**Insight score** = geometric mean of three 1–5 sub-scores: surprise (would a judge expect it?), clarity (readable in 5 seconds?) and relevance (does it support the story: the date decides truth, and our confidence is honest?).
